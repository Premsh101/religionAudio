# Parables & Wisdom Stories

This collection contains **actual story text** in English, Hindi, Arabic and Urdu.

## Editorial standard

Every record is a repository-authored **ORIGINAL_FAITHFUL_RETELLING**. The wording is newly written for the corpus and does not reproduce a modern copyrighted translation.

The collection deliberately separates:

- **Scriptural parables** — stories found in religious scriptures.
- **Canonical teaching stories** — narrative analogies preserved in early religious texts.
- **Rabbinic wisdom** — stories preserved in Jewish rabbinic literature.
- **Classical Sufi literature** — literary teaching stories such as those in Rumi's *Masnavi*.
- **Philosophical teaching narratives** — stories such as the Jain elephant analogy and the Katha Upanishad narrative.
- **Later tradition** — devotional or commentarial stories whose later transmission is explicitly identified.

## Rights and source policy

The source references are used for factual/tradition verification. The repository stores newly authored retellings rather than modern copyrighted translations.

For scripture-associated stories, the canonical reference is recorded without copying a modern translation. For traditional stories, later legendary or devotional elements are labeled rather than presented as independently verified fact.

Before production publication, human review is required for theological terminology, names and transliteration, cultural context, Arabic/Urdu quality, pronunciation/TTS suitability, and source/rights metadata.
