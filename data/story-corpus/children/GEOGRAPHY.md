# Children Corpus — Regional Coverage

| Region / tradition | Initial works |
|---|---|
| UK / USA | Alice; Wizard of Oz; Jungle Book; Just So Stories; Wind in the Willows; Peter Pan; Secret Garden; A Little Princess |
| Germany | Brothers Grimm Household Tales |
| India / Hindi | ईदगाह; दो बैलों की कथा; बड़े भाई साहब |
| Arabic literary tradition | السندباد البحري; كليلة ودمنة |

The target publication languages are always Hindi, English, Arabic and Urdu. Source language and publication language are deliberately separated so culturally different stories can be shared without replacing their original narrative meaning.
