# Inspirational & Resilience Stories

This collection contains **actual story text** in English, Hindi, Arabic and Urdu.

## Editorial standard

Each record is a repository-authored **ORIGINAL_FAITHFUL_RETELLING**. The text is newly written for this corpus and is not copied from a modern copyrighted translation.

Inspirational stories are kept honest:

- **DOCUMENTED_BIOGRAPHY** records follow well-documented facts and, where possible, the subject's own published account (Helen Keller, Frederick Douglass, Booker T. Washington). Their words are paraphrased, not invented.
- **Myth checks** are part of the story where a famous quotation or claim is doubtful (Edison's "ten thousand ways"; the idea that Edison alone invented the light bulb).
- **TRADITIONAL_LEGEND** and **HISTORY_WITH_LEGENDARY_ELEMENTS** records (Robert the Bruce and the spider; Sant Ravidas) label the legendary layer explicitly and say where it first appears.
- Living private individuals are not featured.

## Source and rights policy

Source references in `sourceBasis` were checked to load and are registered in `data/sources/registry.json`. They are used for verification only; their text is not ingested.

Before commercial publication, perform a final human review of:

- biographical facts, dates and figures
- treatment of caste, slavery and segregation in all four languages
- names and transliteration
- Arabic and Urdu language quality
- pronunciation and TTS suitability
