# Inspirational & Resilience Stories — Geography and Coverage

| Story | Main region | Theme | Narrative layer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Robert the Bruce and the Spider | Scotland | Perseverance | Legend (Walter Scott, 1828) framed by documented history |
| Helen Keller and the Word Water | Alabama, USA | Disability, teaching | Autobiography + documented biography |
| Swami Vivekananda at Chicago, 1893 | India and USA | Interfaith respect | Documented biography + published speech |
| Frederick Douglass Learns to Read | Maryland, USA | Literacy, freedom | Autobiography (1845) |
| Booker T. Washington and the Swept Room | Virginia and West Virginia, USA | Education, hard work | Autobiography (1901) |
| Wilma Rudolph | Tennessee, USA; Rome | Disability, sport, civil rights | Documented biography |
| Edison and the Thousand Experiments | USA | Persistence, teamwork | Documented history + myth check |
| Savitribai Phule | Pune, Maharashtra, India | Girls' education, social reform | Documented biography + early accounts |
| Sant Ravidas | Varanasi, India | Equality, devotion | Attested poet-saint + devotional legend |

The set balances Indian reformers and saints with figures from the United States and Europe, and includes stories about caste, slavery, segregation and disability.

Future expansion should add figures from Africa, the Arab world, East Asia and Latin America, more women scientists and educators, and Muslim, Jain and Buddhist resilience stories, always limited to well-documented facts or clearly labelled legend.
