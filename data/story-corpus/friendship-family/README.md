# Friendship, Family & Relationships

This collection contains **actual friendship and family story text** in English, Hindi, Arabic and Urdu, designed for family listening.

## Editorial standard

Every record is a repository-authored **ORIGINAL_FAITHFUL_RETELLING**. The wording is newly written for the corpus and does not reproduce a modern copyrighted translation. Plots follow the named source; nothing is invented.

Records are explicitly separated into:

- **Scriptural narratives**: stories found in sacred texts (Genesis, Samuel, Ruth, the Qur'an, the Bhagavata Purana).
- **Epic episodes**: the Ramayana and Mahabharata, presented as sacred epic literature rather than documented history.
- **Classical anecdotes**: Greek and Roman moral stories such as Damon and Pythias.
- **Documented history with tradition**: the Hijra and the life of Guru Angad, where historical outline, scripture or hadith, and later tradition (sakhis, popular additions) are each labelled.
- **Later folk layers**: for example, the name and kavad image of Shravan Kumar, which are not in Valmiki.

## Respect for sacred figures

- The Prophet Muhammad is not physically described, and no words are invented for him. His words appear only as reported in the Qur'an (9:40) and in hadith, in reported speech.
- The Sikh Gurus are not physically described; Guru Nanak's responses in sakhi episodes are given in reported speech as tradition records them.
- Where Genesis and the Qur'an differ (Joseph and his brothers), both are named and the differences are stated, not merged.
- Morally complex figures (Karna and Duryodhana) are presented as the epic presents them, without excusing wrongdoing.

## Rights and source policy

The repository stores original retellings rather than modern copyrighted translations. Source references are retained for verification and are registered in `data/sources/registry.json`. Short quotations come only from the King James Version (public domain) or, in the Arabic text only, from the Qur'an verbatim.

Before commercial publication, human review is required for:
- theology and religious terminology, including honorifics in each language
- Islamic content (Hijra) by a qualified reviewer
- Sikh content (Guru Angad) by a qualified reviewer
- names and transliteration across four scripts
- Arabic and Urdu language quality
- pronunciation and TTS suitability
