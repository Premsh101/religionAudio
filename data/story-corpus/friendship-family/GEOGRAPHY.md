# Friendship, Family & Relationships: Geography and Tradition Coverage

| Story | Main setting | Relationship | Narrative layer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Krishna and Sudama | Ujjain and Dwarka, India | Friendship | Scriptural narrative (Bhagavata Purana) + later name |
| David and Jonathan | Ancient Israel | Friendship / loyalty | Scriptural narrative (1-2 Samuel) |
| Ruth and Naomi | Moab and Bethlehem | Family / loyalty | Scriptural narrative (Book of Ruth) |
| Damon and Pythias | Syracuse, Sicily | Friendship / trust | Classical anecdote |
| Bharata and Rama's sandals | Ayodhya and Chitrakuta, India | Brothers | Epic episode (Valmiki Ramayana) |
| Joseph forgives his brothers | Canaan and Egypt | Brothers / forgiveness | Scriptural narrative (Genesis and Qur'an) |
| Shravan Kumar | Banks of the Sarayu, India | Parents and child | Epic episode + later folk tradition |
| Abu Bakr and the Hijra | Mecca and Medina, Arabia | Companionship | Qur'an, hadith and documented history |
| Bhai Lehna / Guru Angad | Kartarpur and Khadur Sahib, Punjab | Teacher and disciple | Sikh history + sakhi tradition |
| Karna and Duryodhana | Hastinapura and Kurukshetra, India | Friendship with moral complexity | Epic episode (Mahabharata) |

Coverage includes Hindu, Jewish, Christian, Muslim, Sikh and classical Greek traditions. Future expansion should add Buddhist and Jain friendship stories (friendship Jatakas, the Monkey King's sacrifice), Zoroastrian and Baha'i narratives, sibling stories such as Raksha Bandhan legends, grandparent stories, community and reconciliation narratives from Africa, East Asia and the Americas, and more stories centred on mothers and daughters.
