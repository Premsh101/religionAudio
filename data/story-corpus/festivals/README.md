# Festival Stories

This collection contains **actual festival story text** in English, Hindi, Arabic and Urdu.

## Editorial standard

Every record is a repository-authored **ORIGINAL_FAITHFUL_RETELLING**. The wording is newly written for the corpus and does not reproduce a modern copyrighted translation.

Festival records are explicitly separated into:

- **Scriptural narratives** — stories found in sacred texts.
- **Traditional legends** — narratives used by communities to explain festival meaning.
- **Historical festival accounts** — documented events behind a festival or modern public observance.
- **Cultural / living observance** — where there is no single founding story, the entry explains how the festival is experienced and transmitted.
- **Mixed-layer festivals** — religious story and later historical development are kept as separate layers.

Regional variation is preserved. A festival may have several legitimate stories, so the corpus avoids presenting one regional explanation as the universal meaning of the festival.

## Rights and source policy

The repository stores original retellings rather than modern copyrighted translations. Source references are retained for historical, scriptural, and cultural verification.

For religious claims, the narrative uses language appropriate to the tradition and does not turn theological claims or supernatural events into scientific assertions.

Before commercial publication, human review is required for:
- theology and religious terminology
- names and transliteration
- regional festival differences
- Arabic and Urdu language quality
- pronunciation and TTS suitability
- dates and calendar terminology
- rights metadata for any future direct-source ingestion
