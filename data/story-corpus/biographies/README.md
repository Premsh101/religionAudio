# Biographies & Life Stories

This collection contains **actual biography stories** in English, Hindi, Arabic and Urdu.

## Editorial standard

Every record is a repository-authored **ORIGINAL_FAITHFUL_RETELLING**. It is newly written for the corpus and is not copied from a modern copyrighted translation.

Biography records are separated into evidence layers:

- **Documented biography** — based on institutional or scholarly biographical records.
- **Religious tradition** — where a life is preserved primarily through religious memory and traditional biography.
- **Hagiographic / devotional tradition** — where later devotional accounts add material beyond what can be independently verified.
- **Ancient or mediated biography** — where surviving accounts were written later or by students, disciples, or other authors.

The narrative keeps these distinctions visible and avoids presenting miracles, revelations, disputed dates, or later legends as independently established historical facts.

## Source and rights policy

The corpus does not reproduce modern copyrighted translations. Source references are retained for factual and contextual verification.

Before commercial publication, complete human review of names/transliterations, dates, religious terminology, quotations, historical interpretation, Arabic and Urdu language quality, pronunciation/TTS suitability, and rights metadata.

For religious biographies, the wording intentionally distinguishes **what a tradition believes or remembers** from what can be independently established historically.
