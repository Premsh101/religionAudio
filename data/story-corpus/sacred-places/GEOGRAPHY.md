# Sacred Places & Pilgrimage Stories — Geography and Tradition Coverage

| Place / story | Location | Narrative layer |
|---|---|---|
| Bodh Gaya — the awakening | Bihar, India | Buddhist tradition + Ashokan/5th-6th c. temple history + UNESCO (2002) |
| Lumbini — the Buddha's birth | Rupandehi, Nepal | Buddhist birth tradition + Ashokan pillar inscription + UNESCO (1997) |
| Sarnath — the first sermon | Near Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh, India | Pali Canon + Ashokan monuments + Dhamek Stupa |
| Kashi / Varanasi — city of light | Uttar Pradesh, India | Puranic jyotirlinga story + documented city and temple history |
| Kedarnath — the Pandavas and Shiva | Garhwal Himalaya, Uttarakhand, India | Regional/Puranic legend + temple history + 2013 floods |
| The Kaaba — Ibrahim and Ismail | Makkah, Saudi Arabia | Qur'anic narrative + Islamic tradition + historical rebuilding |
| Western Wall / Temple Mount / al-Haram al-Sharif | Old City of Jerusalem | Documented Herodian/Roman/Umayyad history + Jewish, Muslim and Christian traditions, presented neutrally |
| Harmandir Sahib — four doors | Amritsar, Punjab, India | Documented Sikh history + cherished tradition (Mian Mir, Bibi Rajni) |
| Ajmer Sharif — Khwaja Gharib Nawaz | Ajmer, Rajasthan, India | Historical Sufi saint and shrine + hagiographical tradition |
| Shravanabelagola — Bahubali | Hassan District, Karnataka, India | Jain sacred narrative + 983 CE monolith + inscriptions |
| Santiago de Compostela — Saint James | Galicia, Spain | Acts of the Apostles + medieval legend + documented pilgrimage history |
| Udvada — the Iranshah fire | Valsad District, Gujarat, India | Qissa-i Sanjan + oral tradition + documented 1742 installation |

Traditions covered: Buddhism (3), Hinduism (2), Islam (2 including Sufi), Jewish/Muslim/Christian shared site (1), Sikhism (1), Jainism (1), Christianity (1), Zoroastrianism (1).

Future expansion: Mount Sinai / Saint Catherine's, Nankana Sahib, Pavapuri, Badrinath, Puri, Madurai, Lalibela, Karbala and Najaf, Bethlehem, Hemis and other monasteries, rivers and pilgrimage routes. Contested sites must always present multiple traditions neutrally.
