# Sacred Places & Pilgrimage Stories

This collection contains **actual sacred-place story text** in English, Hindi, Arabic and Urdu.

## Editorial standard

Every record is a repository-authored **ORIGINAL_FAITHFUL_RETELLING**. The wording is newly written for the corpus and does not reproduce a modern copyrighted translation.

Each record tells how a place came to be revered and keeps the layers apart:

- **Documented site history** — construction dates, inscriptions, archaeology, rebuilding and heritage listing.
- **Foundation legends and miracle traditions** — stories communities tell about the origin of the site, marked as tradition.
- **Scriptural narratives** — where a sacred text describes the founding event.
- **Pilgrimage practice** — how people visit and honour the place today.

Religious sensitivity rules applied to every record:
- God is never physically depicted.
- The Prophet Muhammad is never physically described and is given no invented speech.
- Miracles are presented as belief or tradition, not as scientific fact.
- Contested sites (for example Jerusalem's Western Wall / Temple Mount / al-Haram al-Sharif) present each tradition's significance side by side and describe disputes neutrally, without taking a position.

## Rights and source policy

The repository stores original retellings rather than modern copyrighted translations. Source references (see `sourceBasis` on each record and `data/sources/registry.json`) are retained for historical, scriptural and cultural verification.

Before commercial publication, human review is required for:
- theology and religious terminology for each tradition
- neutrality of wording on contested sites
- names, honorifics and transliteration
- dates and historical claims
- Arabic and Urdu language quality
- pronunciation and TTS suitability

The Place model can link these records to place entities directly.
