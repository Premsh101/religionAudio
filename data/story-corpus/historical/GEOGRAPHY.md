# Historical Stories — Geography and Coverage

| Story | Main region | Historical context |
|---|---|---|
| Ashoka After Kalinga | India | Mauryan Empire and Kalinga |
| Lakshmibai and the Defence of Jhansi | India | Indian Rebellion of 1857 |
| Mansa Musa's Journey to Mecca | Mali, North Africa, Arabia | Mali Empire and Hajj |
| Zheng He Reaches the Indian Ocean | China, Southeast Asia, India, Indian Ocean | Ming maritime expeditions |
| Ibn Battuta Arrives in Delhi | Morocco, Arabia, India | Fourteenth-century travel and Tughluq India |
| Joan of Arc and the Relief of Orléans | France | Hundred Years' War |
| Caesar Crosses the Rubicon | Italy / Roman Republic | Roman Civil War, 49 BCE |
| Hannibal Crosses the Alps | North Africa, Iberia, Alps, Italy | Second Punic War, 218 BCE |
| Saladin and Jerusalem, 1187 | Levant / Jerusalem | Crusader-Ayyubid conflict |
| Socrates' Final Day in Athens | Greece | Athenian trial tradition, 399 BCE |

The set deliberately spans South Asia, West Africa, East Asia, the Islamic world, Europe, and the ancient Mediterranean.

Future expansion should add more voices from Africa, Central Asia, Southeast Asia, the Americas, and underrepresented regional histories rather than overloading the collection with European military history.
