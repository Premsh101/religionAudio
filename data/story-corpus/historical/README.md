# Historical Stories

This collection contains **actual story text** in English, Hindi, Arabic and Urdu.

## Editorial standard

Each record is a repository-authored **ORIGINAL_FAITHFUL_RETELLING**. The text is newly written for this corpus and is not copied from a modern copyrighted translation.

Historical material is handled in layers:

- **Documented history** — supported by inscriptions, official historical sources, scholarly reference material or well-established historical records.
- **Travel accounts / ancient narratives** — material survives through authored accounts such as the *Rihla* or ancient writers, so mediation and uncertainty are noted.
- **Legend / devotional tradition** — later religious, heroic or cultural interpretations are not presented as settled historical fact.

Every story keeps the event sequence and central historical meaning while avoiding invented quotations, false precision, and unsupported dramatic details.

## Source and rights policy

The corpus does **not** reproduce modern copyrighted translations. Source references are retained for factual verification. Each record includes an evidence note explaining where uncertainty or later tradition matters.

Before commercial publication, perform a final human review of:

- names and transliteration
- dates and chronology
- historical interpretation
- religious/cultural terminology
- Arabic and Urdu language quality
- pronunciation and TTS suitability
- rights and source metadata for any future canonical-source ingestion
