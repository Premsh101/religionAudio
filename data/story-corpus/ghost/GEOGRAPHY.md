## Product taxonomy

These are deliberately broader than literal ghosts:

- GHOST_STORY: literary supernatural or ghost fiction.
- FOLKLORE: inherited regional supernatural traditions.
- JINN_GHOST_FOLKLORE: Arabic/Islamicate jinn-and-ifrit narratives.
- SUPERNATURAL_FOLKLORE: tales involving spirits, magical beings, uncanny events or enchanted objects.
- SUPERNATURAL_FANTASY: long-form dastan and tilism traditions.

This lets the app distinguish a ghost haunting from a jinn tale, vetala story, banshee tradition, or magical dastan while keeping them in one discovery universe.
