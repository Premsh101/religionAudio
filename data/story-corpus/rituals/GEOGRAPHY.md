# Rituals & Customs Stories — Geography and Tradition Coverage

| Ritual / story | Main setting | Narrative layer |
|---|---|---|
| Sa'i and Zamzam — Hajar's search | Makkah, Arabia | Qur'an + hadith tradition + living Hajj/Umrah practice |
| Passover seder — the four questions | Ancient Egypt narrative; Jewish homes worldwide | Torah narrative + rabbinic Haggadah + living practice |
| Raksha Bandhan — Draupadi and Krishna | India | Popular devotional legend (not classical epic text) + Puranic raksha thread |
| Langar — Guru Nanak's true bargain | Punjab (Nankana Sahib / Chuharkana) | Janamsakhi tradition + documented Sikh institution |
| Alms round — the Buddha in Kapilavastu | Kapilavastu; South and Southeast Asia | Pali commentarial tradition + living monastic practice |
| Mezuzah — Onkelos and the doorpost | Ancient Israel; Roman-era story | Torah command + Talmudic narrative + living practice |
| Breaking of bread — Last Supper and Emmaus | Jerusalem / Emmaus | Gospel narrative + Christian belief + varied church practice |
| Baptism — Jesus at the Jordan | Jordan River, Judea | Gospel narrative + historical John the Baptist + varied church practice |
| Navjote — sudreh and kusti | Ancient Iran; Parsi India and diaspora | Living practice with traditional symbolism; origins uncertain |
| Pitru Paksha — Karna's golden meal | India | Popular legend + shraddha described in ancient texts |
| Akshaya Tritiya — Rishabhanatha's first alms | Hastinapura (Jain tradition) | Jain sacred narrative + living varshitap practice |
| Sadaqah — the owners of the garden | Arabia (Qur'anic narrative) | Qur'an + tafsir details + living zakat/sadaqah practice |

Traditions covered: Hinduism (2), Islam (2), Judaism (2), Christianity (2), Sikhism (1), Buddhism (1), Jainism (1), Zoroastrianism (1).

Future expansion should add life-cycle customs (weddings, naming, funerals), fasting traditions, and regional variants of the same ritual rather than presenting one community's practice as universal.
