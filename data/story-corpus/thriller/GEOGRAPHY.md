# Thriller Corpus — Regional Coverage

| Region / tradition | Stories |
|---|---|
| Britain / Ireland | Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde; Dracula; Frankenstein; The Woman in White; The Moonstone; The Thirty-Nine Steps; The Lodger; The Island of Doctor Moreau |
| India / Hindi | चन्द्रकान्ता; चन्द्रकान्ता सन्तति |
| India / Urdu | فردوس بریں; فسانہ آزاد |
| Arabic literary tradition | مدينة النحاس (ADVENTURE); حكاية الفرس الأبنوس |

The corpus intentionally mixes psychological suspense, Gothic thriller, conspiracy, espionage, adventure, fantasy and survival. The label describes narrative form; it does not assert that supernatural material is scientifically factual.
