# Survival & Disaster Stories — Geography and Coverage

| Story | Main setting | Survival type | Narrative layer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Noah and the Ark | Ancient Near East | Flood | Genesis + Quran (shared tradition) |
| Manu and the Fish | India | Flood | Shatapatha Brahmana + Puranic layer |
| Jonah and the Great Fish | Joppa to Nineveh / the sea | Lost at sea | Book of Jonah + Quran |
| Utnapishtim and the Flood Tablet | Mesopotamia (Iraq); London | Flood | Ancient literature + documented 1872 discovery |
| Yusuf and the Seven Years | Ancient Egypt | Famine planning | Genesis + Quran |
| Shackleton and the Endurance | Antarctica, Weddell Sea, South Georgia | Polar endurance, open-boat voyage | Documented history |
| Apollo 13 | Space between Earth and Moon | Technical emergency | Documented history (NASA records) |
| The Chilean Miners | Atacama Desert, Chile | Trapped underground, rescue | Documented history |
| The Little Ships of Dunkirk | France and the English Channel | Mass rescue at sea | Documented history |

The three flood narratives from three traditions (Abrahamic, Hindu, Mesopotamian) are intentionally placed together so listeners can compare a shared human theme without treating any one as scientific fact.

Future expansion should add South Asian and African documented rescues (once sourced to official or archival material), wilderness and desert survival, and Pacific and Arctic Indigenous survival traditions.
