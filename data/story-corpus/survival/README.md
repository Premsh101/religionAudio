# Survival & Disaster Stories

This collection contains **actual story text** in English, Hindi, Arabic and Urdu.

## Editorial standard

Each record is a repository-authored **ORIGINAL_FAITHFUL_RETELLING**. The text is newly written for this corpus and is not copied from a modern copyrighted translation.

The collection prefers **hopeful survival and rescue**: preparation, teamwork, fairness, patience and faith, rather than suffering for its own sake.

Records are separated into:

- **SCRIPTURAL_NARRATIVE** — Noah/Nuh, Jonah/Yunus, Joseph/Yusuf (Bible and Quran told side by side, with differences preserved) and Manu and the fish (Shatapatha Brahmana, with later Puranic layers marked). Miraculous elements are presented as sacred narrative and belief, never as scientific or geological fact.
- **TRADITIONAL_LEGEND** — Utnapishtim's flood from the Epic of Gilgamesh, presented as ancient literature, alongside the documented 1872 discovery of the tablet.
- **HISTORICAL_EVENT** — Shackleton's Endurance expedition, Apollo 13, the 2010 Chilean mine rescue and the Dunkirk Little Ships, following documented records without invented dialogue.

Living private individuals (for example, most of the Chilean miners) are not named.

## Source and rights policy

Source references in `sourceBasis` were checked to load and are registered in `data/sources/registry.json`. They are used for verification only; their text is not ingested.

Before commercial publication, perform a final human review of:

- theology and scriptural terminology in all four languages (including Quranic paraphrase)
- names, transliteration and dates
- Arabic and Urdu language quality
- pronunciation and TTS suitability
- age-band suitability for disaster content
