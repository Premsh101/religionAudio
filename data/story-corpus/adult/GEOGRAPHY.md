# Adult Romance Stories — Geography and Tradition Coverage

| Story | Main setting | Narrative layer |
|---|---|---|
| Bilhana and the Princess | Kashmir / North India | Sanskrit literature + legendary frame story |
| Roopmati and Baz Bahadur | Mandu, Malwa (Madhya Pradesh) | Documented history + regional legend |
| Bajirao and Mastani | Pune and Bundelkhand | Documented Maratha history + later dramatisation |
| Anarkali and Salim | Lahore (Mughal court) | Legend with disputed historical basis |
| Khosrow and Shirin | Persia (Iran) and Armenia | Persian literature (Nizami) + Sasanian historical core |
| Jamil and Buthayna | Hijaz (Arabia) and Egypt | Historical poet + classical Arabic biographical tradition |
| Xuanzong and Yang Guifei | Chang'an and Mawei, China | Tang history + Bai Juyi's poem |
| Antony and Cleopatra | Tarsus, Alexandria, Actium | Ancient history (Plutarch) |
| Tristan and Iseult | Cornwall, Ireland, Brittany | Medieval romance / legend |
| Paolo and Francesca | Ravenna and Rimini, Italy | Historical event + Dante and Boccaccio |

Coverage: South Asia (4), Persianate world (1), Arab world (1), East Asia (1), Mediterranean / Europe (3).

Future expansion could add Japanese (adult-only episodes of court literature with clearly adult protagonists), Ottoman, Andalusian, African and Latin American romances, always keeping the same 18+, non-explicit and no-sacred-figures rules.
