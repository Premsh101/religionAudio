# War & Courage Stories

This collection contains **actual story text** in English, Hindi, Arabic and Urdu.

## Editorial standard

Each record is a repository-authored **ORIGINAL_FAITHFUL_RETELLING**. The text is newly written for this corpus and is not copied from a modern copyrighted translation.

The subject of this collection is **courage, not violence**:

- Battles are described without graphic detail, and no side is glorified.
- Courage includes moral courage: refusing to give up one's conscience, standing up for another community's freedom, making peace, resisting without weapons, and admitting doubt.
- Where a story involves communities that have been in conflict (Crusader/Ayyubid, Mughal/Sikh, Delhi Sultanate/Rajput), the text attributes actions to specific rulers or states, gives context fairly, and avoids communal blame.

Every record separates its layers in text and in `evidenceType` / `evidenceNote`:

- **HISTORICAL_EVENT / DOCUMENTED_BIOGRAPHY** — supported by contemporary records, official documents or well-established historical scholarship.
- **HISTORY_WITH_LEGENDARY_ELEMENTS** — a documented core with later chronicle anecdotes, bardic tradition or literary retellings, each labelled as such.
- **SCRIPTURAL_NARRATIVE** — told as sacred scripture of the relevant tradition, not as verified history.

No quotations are invented for real people. The Prophet Muhammad is referred to respectfully and is not described or given dialogue.

## Age guidance

Age bands are deliberately conservative (mostly 10+ or 12+) because the stories involve war, persecution and death, even though none of it is described graphically.

## Source and rights policy

Source references in `sourceBasis` were checked to load and are registered in `data/sources/registry.json`. They are used for verification only; their text is not ingested.

Before commercial publication, perform a final human review of:

- historical interpretation and communal sensitivity (especially the Sikh, Islamic and Rajasthan records)
- names, transliteration and religious honorifics
- dates and chronology
- Arabic and Urdu language quality
- pronunciation and TTS suitability
- age-band suitability
