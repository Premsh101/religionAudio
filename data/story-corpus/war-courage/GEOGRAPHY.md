# War & Courage Stories — Geography and Coverage

| Story | Main region | Context | Narrative layer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Saladin and Richard at Jaffa, 1192 | Levant / Palestine | Third Crusade and Treaty of Jaffa | Documented history + chronicle anecdotes |
| Thermopylae | Greece | Greco-Persian Wars, 480 BCE | Herodotus + later legend |
| David and Goliath | Ancient Israel | 1 Samuel 17 / Quran 2:251 | Scripture shared by three traditions |
| Guru Tegh Bahadur | Punjab and Delhi, India | Mughal India, 1675 | Documented history + Sikh historical tradition |
| Bilal ibn Rabah | Mecca and Medina, Arabia | Early Islam | Early Islamic biographical sources |
| Noor Inayat Khan | France, Britain (Indian heritage) | Second World War | Documented biography |
| Chittorgarh, 1303 | Mewar, Rajasthan, India | Delhi Sultanate | Documented siege + Jayasi's Padmavat (1540) |
| Arjuna's Dilemma | Kurukshetra, India | Mahabharata / Bhagavad Gita | Hindu scripture |
| Badshah Khan | North-West Frontier (today Pakistan) | Indian independence movement | Documented biography |

The set spans South Asia, the Middle East and Europe and includes Hindu, Sikh, Muslim, Jewish and Christian traditions as well as secular history. Four of the nine stories centre on non-violent or moral courage.

Future expansion should add Africa, East and Southeast Asia and the Americas (for example, resistance and peacemaking figures), and more women's courage stories, while keeping graphic violence out and keeping history, scripture and legend clearly separated.
