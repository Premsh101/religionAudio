# Romance & Love Stories: Geography and Tradition Coverage

| Story | Main setting | Narrative layer |
|---|---|---|
| Layla and Majnun | Arabia (Persian poem) | Classical literature (Nizami) on earlier Arabic tradition |
| Heer and Ranjha | Punjab, Chenab valley | Folk romance (Waris Shah's qissa) |
| Shirin and Farhad | Iran / Armenia; retold in South Asia | Classical literature (Nizami) + South Asian folk ending |
| Sohni and Mahiwal | Punjab / Sindh | Folk romance + Sufi poetry (Shah Abdul Latif) |
| Sassi and Punnu | Sindh / Balochistan | Folk romance + Sufi poetry (Shah Abdul Latif) |
| Jacob and Rachel | Haran and Canaan | Scriptural narrative (Genesis) |
| Yusuf and Zulaikha | Egypt (setting); Persia (poem) | Qur'anic narrative + Persian Sufi poem (Jami) |
| Shakuntala and Dushyanta | Himalayan foothills, India | Classical Sanskrit drama (Kalidasa) on a Mahabharata episode |
| Mirabai | Rajasthan, Vrindavan, Dwarka | History + devotional hagiography and poetry |
| Dhola and Maru | Rajasthan (Narwar and Pugal) | Rajasthani folk ballad |

Coverage spans South Asia (Punjab, Sindh, Balochistan, Rajasthan, the Gangetic plain), Persia and the Arab world, and the ancient Near East. Future expansion should add Mirza-Sahiban, Sassi-Punnu variants from Punjab, Roop-Basant and other regional Indian romances, Bengali and Tamil love narratives, Song of Songs readings, and East Asian and African traditions. Regional variants should be added as separate records rather than merged into one canonical version.
