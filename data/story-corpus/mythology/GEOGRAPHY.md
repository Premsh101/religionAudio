# Mythology Corpus — Regional Coverage

| Tradition | Initial coverage |
|---|---|
| Greek / Roman | Prometheus, Pandora, Apollo, Daphne, Midas, Cupid, Psyche, Perseus, Medusa, Golden Fleece, Hercules, Orpheus, Eurydice, Ulysses |
| Norse | Creation, Thor, Baldur, Loki, Ragnarok |
| Ancient Egyptian | Creation, Destruction of Mankind, Ra and Snake-Bite, Horus, Origin of Horus, Isis and Osiris |
| Hindu / Indian | Savitri and Satyavan, Nala and Damayanti, Draupadi's Swayamvara; the Bhagavad-Gita is catalogued here as SCRIPTURE |
| Buddhist | (Jataka fables moved to the children corpus) |
| Welsh | The Lady of the Fountain, Branwen |
| Finnish / Karelian | The Sampo, Creation cycle |

Every story is intended for publication in Hindi, English, Arabic and Urdu.

Regional expansion should prioritize traditions with strong open historical editions and clearly documented provenance.
